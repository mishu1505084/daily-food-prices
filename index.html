Prices are Shwapno's online prices, collected daily at 9 AM Bangladesh time. Nutrients are typical reference values for the edible part, raw (USDA FoodData Central and the Bangladesh Food Composition Table), not Shwapno's labels. "Per ৳100 spent" removes bones, heads, peel and seeds first, so fish and meat with bone compare fairly with boneless cuts.
