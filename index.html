Prices are Shwapno's online prices, after any discount, collected daily at 9 AM Bangladesh time. "Edible" removes bones, heads, peel and seeds, so fish and meat with bone are compared fairly with boneless cuts. Download: today's prices (CSV) · price history (JSON).
